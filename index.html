Privacy Policy

True or Lie: Party Game (also called TrueOrLie AI)

Effective date: 12 September 2026
Android package: com.dubnickiy.trueorlieai
Developer: Lucky Goose
Privacy contact: gunnarsonice@gmail.com

This is an entertainment game. It does not detect lies, identify people, verify the meaning of answers, or assess health. A displayed percentage describes recording quality, not truthfulness.

This policy explains how the Android app and this policy page handle information. Some features, including purchases, may be unavailable in testing builds. Descriptions of optional features apply when you use those features.

1. Camera, microphone and device inputs

With your permission, the camera provides a live preview and MediaPipe processes face landmarks and movement on your device while the scan screen is active. Raw camera video and face landmarks are not saved. The app does not use this processing to identify you or create a face-recognition profile.

The microphone measures sound volume and records a short answer during a round. Answer audio is saved in private app storage for history playback. Summarized movement, voice and recording-quality measurements are included in saved results. We do not upload camera images or answer recordings to a developer-operated server, provide them to advertisers, or use them to train a remote AI model.

The app also reads available audio-device information to display and select microphone or headset routing. Camera and microphone access is released when the relevant screen is no longer active. Record only willing participants and obtain their permission first.

2. Local information and retention

The app stores up to 50 recent saved rounds, including questions, timestamps, playful results, signal measurements and available answer recordings. It also stores custom questions, language and theme preferences, scan allowances, reward receipts and completed-round identifiers needed to prevent duplicate rewards or charges.

Free results may be retained while their history view is locked. Locking history or ending a subscription does not delete saved information. Older rounds and their recordings are removed as newer rounds replace them beyond the 50-round limit.

These records are kept in Android private app storage. History and answer audio are excluded from the app's configured Android cloud backups and device transfers. The app does not offer developer-operated cloud synchronization or a user-account system.

3. Your deletion and permission controls

  • Use Clear history to remove saved rounds and their associated audio, including without Premium. Individual deletion is available when you can open the history.
  • Clearing history does not delete custom questions, preferences or scan/reward bookkeeping. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → this app → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • You can refuse or revoke camera and microphone permissions in Android settings. Features requiring those inputs will be unavailable.
  • We cannot retrieve or remotely erase recordings kept only on your device. Contact us for help using the deletion controls.

4. Optional rewarded advertising

If you choose to load a rewarded advertisement, Google AdMob processes information to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. This may include your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, advertising and app/device identifiers, applicable account-related identifiers, interactions such as taps and video views, and diagnostic/performance information. Google describes Mobile Ads SDK transmissions as protected with TLS encryption. Camera images and answer audio are not supplied by the app as advertising data.

Test advertisements also contact Google. Where required, production advertisements use Google's consent flow; a privacy-options control is available in app settings when Google's consent service requires it. You may decline rewarded ads, and Android settings provide controls for resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Google processes and retains its service data under its own policies.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play

When purchases are enabled, Google Play handles checkout, payment details and transaction records. The app receives purchase information such as product identifiers, purchase tokens, acknowledgement and purchase status to check Premium access, acknowledge purchases and restore access. The app does not receive payment-card numbers and currently has no developer-operated purchase-verification server.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription or erase Google's payment records. Retention of Google's transaction records is governed by Google's policies and applicable obligations.

6. Question narration and sharing

Reading a question aloud sends its text to the Android text-to-speech service selected on your device. Depending on that service and voice, the text may be processed remotely under the provider's policy. Avoid entering sensitive information into questions you ask the service to read.

If you choose Share, the app sends the question and result text to the app you select through Android's share sheet. Answer audio is not attached by this sharing feature. The receiving app handles the shared text under its own policy.

7. Contact messages and this website

If you email Lucky Goose, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your enquiry and handle privacy requests, retaining correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Do not send answer recordings or other sensitive information unless necessary for your request. Email is handled through Gmail under Google's policies.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or forms. GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google and GitHub may process information in countries other than your own.

8. Security, requests and children

Local information is protected by Android's app-storage access controls and your device's security settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and use the available deletion controls.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold. Send requests to gunnarsonice@gmail.com. For information stored only in the app, use the local controls above; for information controlled by Google or GitHub, their policies explain available controls and request mechanisms.

Parents or guardians with concerns about a child's use of the app or information sent to us can contact the same address. Do not record children or other people without the permissions required for your situation. This policy does not replace the app's age rating or parental controls.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Where additional notice or consent is required for a change, it will be provided before the relevant processing.
